Where It Began

A kitchen that feels like home

In Norway there is a word for warmth, calm and contentment: kos. It is exactly what we set out to bring to the modern kitchen. Less noise, more quality, and tools that are a pleasure to hold.

Every piece we carry must answer three simple questions. Is it made of something good? Will it last for years? And is it beautiful even when it is simply resting on the counter?
