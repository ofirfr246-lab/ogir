Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about orders, delivery, returns and caring for your pieces.

Orders & Payment

Which payment methods do you accept?

In a live store: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and PayPal. This demo store takes no payment.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. Orders over $150 can be split into four interest-free payments (live store only).

How will I know my order was received?

You will receive a confirmation email with your order number and a tracking link right away.

Shipping

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days in the contiguous US; Express takes 1–2 business days.

How much is shipping?

Standard shipping is $7.99 and complimentary on orders over $75.

Do you ship internationally?

We currently ship within the United States. International shipping is coming soon.

Returns & Warranty

What is your return policy?

Unused items in their original packaging may be returned within 30 days for a full refund.

What does the warranty cover?

Cast iron carries a 10-year warranty and small appliances a 2-year warranty. Details are on each product page.

My item arrived damaged. What should I do?

Send us a photo via the contact page and we will ship a replacement at no cost.

Care

How do I care for cast iron?

Rinse with hot water, dry thoroughly over low heat and wipe on a thin layer of oil. Never leave it to soak.

Are the wooden boards dishwasher safe?

No. Hand wash and oil monthly with food-safe mineral oil to keep the wood nourished.

Is the stoneware microwave safe?

Most of our stoneware is dishwasher and microwave safe, as noted in each product's specifications.
