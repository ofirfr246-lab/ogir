Shipping & Returns

A simple, transparent policy, so you can shop with complete peace of mind.

Free shipping over $75

Below that, standard delivery is a flat $7.99.

3–5 business days

Contiguous US. Express delivery in 1–2 business days.

30-day returns

Free returns on unused items in original packaging.

Shipping

  • Orders placed before 1pm ET on a business day ship the same day.
  • A tracking link is emailed as soon as your order leaves our studio.
  • We ship to all 50 states; Alaska and Hawaii may take 2–3 extra days.
  • Heavy items (over 30 lb) may arrive in a separate shipment.

Returns & exchanges

  • Return any unused item within 30 days of delivery in its original packaging.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days of receipt.
  • Damaged or incorrect items are replaced at our expense, including return pickup.
